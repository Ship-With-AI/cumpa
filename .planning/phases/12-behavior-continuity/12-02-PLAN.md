<objective>
Close the one genuine CON-03 coverage gap: sequential Tab traversal of the review toolbar.

Purpose: CON-03 asks for accessibility re-checked wherever markup structurally changed — focus reachability, roles, names, and keyboard operability. Playwright's role engine already carries almost all of that matrix. Across the six ROADMAP-named specs there are 290 `getByRole` calls and 37 `toBeFocused` assertions, already covering aria-level, aria-selected, aria-expanded, roving tabindex, treeitem/tree/navigation roles, aria-modal, aria-haspopup, and every named dialog control. No new dependency is warranted — an axe-style generic scanner finds different defects than a named-role contract matrix, and this contract is a named-role matrix.

What is actually missing is the Tab-order traversal: the UI-SPEC requires that in a middle-file fixture all six review-toolbar controls are enabled and that sequential Tab visits them in order, and nothing in the suite asserts the hop sequence.

Purpose (second half): while writing that step you will notice the toolbar's containers carry `aria-label` on bare `div`s with no `role`, so the named groups are dropped from the accessibility tree. Record it; do not fix it. `12-UI-SPEC.md:182` forbids Phase 12 implementing it, and the git evidence shows it did not regress during the restyle.

Output: one new `test.step` and one written finding.
</objective>
